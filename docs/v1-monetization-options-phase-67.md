# LiftPDF Phase 67 - Monetization Options

Date: 2026-08-12

No monetization was implemented during Phase 67.

## Constraints

LiftPDF's strongest V1 differentiator is browser-local processing. Monetization must not quietly introduce uploads, tracking, or dark patterns.

## Options

| Option | Fit | Revenue potential | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Non-intrusive ads | Medium | Medium | Medium | Consider only after traffic baseline; avoid tool UI clutter. |
| Sponsorship on Learning Center | Medium | Medium | Low/Medium | Possible if sponsors are relevant and clearly labeled. |
| Pro browser features | Medium | Medium | Medium | Only if features remain local and clearly useful. |
| Team/admin backend | Low for V1 | High | High | Defer; changes privacy/security/compliance scope. |
| API product | Low for V1 | Medium/High | High | Defer; different product and infrastructure. |
| Affiliate links in guides | Medium | Low/Medium | Medium | Use only where genuinely helpful and disclosed. |
| Donations/supporter plan | Medium | Low | Low | Safe, but not a growth lever. |
| Paid desktop/offline app | Medium | Medium | Medium | Interesting if privacy-first audience appears. |

## Sale Readiness

Current classification: NOT_READY_FOR_SALE_AS_BUSINESS

Reason:

- V1 product is launch-ready.
- Traffic/revenue baseline is not established.
- GA4 and Search Console trend data are not yet collected.
- Monetization model is not proven.

Potentially ready for acquisition discussion after:

- 30-90 days of traffic data;
- conversion funnel evidence;
- retention/repeat-use signals;
- revenue experiment or strong organic growth;
- documented operational stability.

## Recommended First Experiment

Do not monetize immediately. First 30 days should focus on:

1. real user acquisition;
2. tool funnel conversion;
3. error monitoring;
4. Search Console opportunity;
5. repeat-use evidence.
